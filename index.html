Coffee is a Culture!

From passion to a full repair workshop.

Coffee CPR started as a result of waiting to have my own business. I have worked many years in the field, which led me to meet so many people not only interested in repairing their machines but also buying the best coffee around. With such high demand, I created my personal brand of coffee, Tipicoffee, a few years back, which eventually led me to start Coffee CPR—because you can't just sell coffee, it's also necessary to provide the best Italian coffee machines, as well as the best repair service.

Today, our slogan "Giving your machine a second shot!" represents our core vow: combining technical precision, genuine spare parts, and deep coffee expertise to extend the life of home and commercial coffee gear across the region.
